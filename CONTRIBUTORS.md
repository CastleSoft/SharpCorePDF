## How to appear here

A report is a contribution when it can be acted on: the version you ran, the smallest snippet that
reproduces the behaviour, and what you expected instead. Those reports are what turn "something is wrong"
into a red test and a fix - see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
